# IusMorfos Framework - Conceptual Overview

## Theoretical Foundation

Legal norms as replicating units of information (Dawkins' memetics):
- **Vertical**: Legislative adoption
- **Horizontal**: Jurisprudential citation  
- **Cross-jurisdictional**: International harmonization

## The 12 Dimensions

### 1. Temporal
Evolution of norms over time

### 2. Normative  
Hierarchical legal structure

### 3. Jurisdictional
Geographic scope and conflicts

### 4. Doctrinal
Theoretical foundations

### 5. Procedural
Process requirements

### 6. Sanction
Enforcement mechanisms

### 7. Rights-Based
Protected interests

### 8. Institutional
Organizational actors

### 9. Economic
Financial implications

### 10. Technological
Digital transformation

### 11. Cultural
Social context

### 12. Implementation
Law vs practice gap

## Applications

- Cross-jurisdictional comparative law
- Regulatory compliance mapping  
- Legal risk assessment
- Implementation gap analysis
